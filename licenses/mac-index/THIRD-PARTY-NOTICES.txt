Third-party notices for native/crates/ops-mac-index (shipped with dsh-ops)

These crates are cargo dependencies of ops-mac-index at the exact versions
recorded in native/Cargo.lock. The dependency is target-gated to
cfg(target_os = "macos"), so it is only built for the macOS worker, but the
license obligation follows the source distribution and is recorded here anyway.

Where a package offers a choice between MIT and Apache-2.0, the MIT text is
reproduced below and MIT is the selected alternative. Versions and publication
dates are recorded in docs/mac-index-dependencies.md.
